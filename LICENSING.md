## Historical boundary

Releases through `v0.3.1` were published under the historical product name
Scanora and under the Apache License 2.0. Those grants remain valid for those
versions. Existing commits, tags, release assets, and license text in their
historical Git states are intentionally preserved.

commit. They do not revoke or rewrite rights already granted for historical
releases. The exact transition commit is recorded in the `v0.4.0` release notes
and changelog once the commit exists.

## Ownership audit

The repository history was reviewed on 2026-08-31 with `git shortlog -sne
--all` and a unique-author scan. The human author identities found belong to
the project owner; the remaining author is the Dependabot automation account.
No external human contribution requiring a separate relicensing agreement was
identified in the current history.

This is a repository-history audit, not a claim that third-party dependencies
or assets are owned by Soturine. Their terms remain independent.
