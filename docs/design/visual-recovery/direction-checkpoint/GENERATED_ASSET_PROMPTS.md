## A — rosto + captura

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: original alternate ReplicaScan Android adaptive launcher icon artwork with genuinely transparent background. Reference image is the approved mascot identity, not an edit target. Make a close, compact portrait of the SAME charming fox: orange, rounded cream cheeks, soft dark-tipped ears, large bright brown eyes, turquoise bandana. Frame the face between two subtle teal scanner-corner marks, with one small sheet of paper below the chin. Warm semi-dimensional painterly icon finish matching the reference character. A distinctive clean silhouette readable at 48dp, centered with 20% padding to survive adaptive icon masks. This alternate concept emphasizes the expressive face, with scanning marks secondary. No lettering, no background square, no multiple icons, no photoreal fur, no gender styling, no flat geometric vector, no watermark.

## B — folha iluminada

> Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: ReplicaScan Android adaptive launcher icon concept B, transparent central artwork. Reference: approved fox master for character identity. Depict the SAME small kawaii fox, orange fur, cream rounded cheeks, lively brown eyes, dark ear tips and turquoise bandana, sitting behind a single cream document page whose top edge is just below its smiling muzzle. The page is gently illuminated as if it is being scanned, with only a thin warm amber scan line across the page. Make the character and page form one uncluttered cohesive symbol in warm painterly semi-dimensional style matching reference. Icon safe area: keep all essential details inside central 70% of square for circle/squircle cropping. Readable at 48dp. Do not add text, app frame, surrounding square, additional characters, photo texture, realistic fur, feminine styling, flat SVG appearance, or multiple alternatives.

Ambos precisam de inspeção em tamanho real, máscaras adaptativas, versão
monocromática e aprovação antes de integrar.
